About us

Rooted in Chapakot

Ishara Agriculture Multipurpose Farm is run by Ishwor Acharya on 41 ropani of land in Chapakot, Gandaki Province. The farm combines fish farming, beekeeping, shiitake mushroom cultivation, duck rearing, fruit, spices and Amriso broom grass.

By working across several farming activities, we make good use of our land and water, and offer a wider range of locally produced goods to customers in Nepal.

  • Fish ponds for Pangasius and carp
  • Serena (Apis cerana) beehives for honey
  • Japanese shiitake mushroom cultivation
  • Pekin-cross and Mallard ducks
  • Lemon, Nibuwa, spices and Amriso

Visitors and buyers are welcome to arrange a farm visit in advance by phone or WhatsApp.
