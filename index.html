About us

Rooted in Chapakot

Ishara Agriculture Multipurpose Farm is run by Ishwor Acharya on 41 ropani of land in Chapakot, Gandaki Province. The farm combines aquaculture, beekeeping, mushroom cultivation, livestock and crop farming.

By working across several farming activities, we make good use of our land and water, and offer a wider range of locally produced goods to customers in Nepal.

  • Fish ponds for aquaculture
  • Beehives for honey production
  • Shiitake mushroom cultivation
  • Ducks and livestock
  • Fruit, Amriso, spices and field crops

Visitors and buyers are welcome to arrange a farm visit in advance by phone or WhatsApp.
